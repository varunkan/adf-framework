# ANDS Studio — synthetic usability panel (SSR method)

## Qualitative themes per flow

### AI-assisted document drafting chat (ANDS dossier builder)

Here are the dominant themes, ordered by frequency:

1. **Lack of Control and Configuration Options** (7)
   - Short name: Limited Control
   - Representative quote: "I'd like to see a way for users to configure or request configuration without relying on an admin."
   - Concrete product change: Add a self-service configuration option for the AI backend, allowing users to configure it without needing administrative support.

2. **Transparency and Visibility** (6)
   - Short name: Transparency
   - Representative quote: "I'd like to see more explicit warnings and alerts for users who choose to use the AI drafting feature."
   - Concrete product change: Display a prominent warning when the AI backend is not configured, and provide clear instructions on how to configure it.

3. **Audit Trail and Provenance** (5)
   - Short name: Audit Trail
   - Representative quote: "I'd like to see more visibility into the audit trail and the ability to track changes made by the AI."
   - Concrete product change: Display a clear and prominent audit trail and provenance information, allowing users to easily verify the AI-assisted draft's compliance.

4. **Flexibility and Customization** (4)
   - Short name: Flexibility
   - Representative quote: "I'd like to see more flexibility in how I interact with the AI, such as the ability to review and edit the AI's output before it's saved."
   - Concrete product change: Add the ability to review and edit AI-generated content before saving, and provide more customization options for the AI's output.

5. **Guidance and Training** (3)
   - Short name: Guidance
   - Representative quote: "I'd like to see more training or guidance on how to use the AI effectively."
   - Concrete product change: Provide more explicit guidance on how to use the AI, including tutorials and examples, to help users get the most out of the feature.

6. **Warning and Alert System** (3)
   - Short name: Warning System
   - Representative quote: "I'd like to see more explicit warnings and alerts for users who choose to use the AI drafting feature."
   - Concrete product change: Implement a clear and prominent warning system that alerts users to potential issues, such as unconfigured AI backend or AI-generated content that hasn't been reviewed.

### Validate the dossier and export the eCTD package

**Theme 1: Validation and Error Handling (14)**

- **Name:** Reliable Validation
- **Respondents:** 14
- **Quote:** "I'd change the 'Export anyway' prompt to a more explicit warning about the consequences of exporting an invalid package."
- **Concrete Change:** Display a prominent warning when the validator is unreachable, and make re-validation a mandatory step before export.

**Theme 2: User Guidance and Feedback (10)**

- **Name:** Clear Guidance
- **Respondents:** 10
- **Quote:** "I'd like to see more explicit guidance on how to interpret the rule codes and messages, especially for less common or more complex validation issues."
- **Concrete Change:** Provide detailed explanations of terms and options used, and offer examples or links to Health Canada resources for clarification.

**Theme 3: Customization and Flexibility (8)**

- **Name:** Customization Options
- **Respondents:** 8
- **Quote:** "I'd like to see more flexibility in customizing the validation process or export options to suit specific needs."
- **Concrete Change:** Allow users to customize the validation settings, such as which rules to apply, and offer more options for customizing the workspace layout and section visibility.

**Theme 4: Health Canada ID and Validation Requirements (6)**

- **Name:** ID and Validation Requirements
- **Respondents:** 6
- **Quote:** "I'd change the 'No ID yet?' prompt to something more explicit, such as 'Please enter a valid Health Canada dossier ID (e.g. e123456) or click 'No ID yet?' to proceed with a placeholder ID.'"
- **Concrete Change:** Display a clear warning or alert when creating a dossier without a valid Health Canada ID, and provide more explicit guidance on the dossier ID requirements.

**Theme 5: Export and Validation Process (5)**

- **Name:** Export and Validation Process
- **Respondents:** 5
- **Quote:** "I'd like to see more prominent warnings about the need for a valid Health Canada ID and the potential consequences of invalid submissions."
- **Concrete Change:** Display a prominent warning when the validator is unreachable, and make re-validation a mandatory step before export.

**Theme 6: Layout and Organization (4)**

- **Name:** Layout and Organization
- **Respondents:** 4
- **Quote:** "I like the organized layout and the clear status indicators for validation and export."
- **Concrete Change:** Improve the 'Sequences' section, and offer more flexibility in customizing the workspace layout and section visibility.
