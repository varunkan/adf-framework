## Validation & Export  (`validate_export`)
**Highest-leverage:** Item 1 (ruleset version/provenance/staleness) is the clear lever: raised in 14 of 24 responses by 8 of 12 personas, and it is the explicit adoption precondition for the senior buyers — QA managers, RA directors, CDMO managers, and publishers — who call a silently stale catalogue "worse than none" and say they "can't defend" CA-E ids without the exact HC guidance/spec version behind them. The fix is cheap relative to payoff: a pinned provenance block, a staleness warning, and stamping the version into the exported report ride on data the product already holds ("Ruleset synced: [date]").
