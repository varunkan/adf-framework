- Validate that both bill amount and tip percent are positive numbers, preventing zero or negative values.  
- Compute the tip amount (bill × tip / 100) and total (bill + tip) using floating‑point arithmetic with sufficient precision.  
- Divide equally among specified people, handling remainders by distributing one extra unit to as many people as possible.  
- Present the results in a clear format showing individual amounts and totals for each participant.
