          total. Uses the bill, tip, tax, and people above.

Loyalty rewards

        
Earn points on the pre-tax subtotal and optionally redeem
          banked points against the total (like store credit). Tax and tip still
          apply to the full bill above; only the leftover balance is split.

        

          
          
        

        

          
          
          
        

        

      


      

        
Tipping by country

        
Travelling? Pick a destination to apply the tip
          percentage that's customary there for sit-down service. Reuses the
          bill / tax / people inputs above and splits the same way.

        

          
          
        

        

      


      

        
Large-party gratuity

        
Many restaurants add an automatic gratuity once a party
          reaches a set size. The auto rate only applies when the people count
          above hits the threshold; you can still add an extra voluntary tip on
          top. Reuses the bill / tax / people inputs above.

        

          
          
          
          
        

        

      


      

        
Comp a diner

        
Covering someone's meal? List the comped diner numbers
          (1-based, comma-separated); the rest split the whole bill above.

        

          
          
        

        

      


      

        
Split with shared items

        
Each diner's own items, one line per person (commas for
          several items). Shared dishes are split evenly across the table; tip and
          tax use the tip% / tax% inputs above.

        

          
        

        

          
          
        

        

      


      

        
Assign items to diners

        
Itemise the check and say who shared each line. One item
          per line as amount diners, where diners are 1-based
          seat numbers (e.g. 30 1 2 = a $30 dish split by seats 1 & 2).
          Omit the seats to share a line across the whole table. Tip %, tax % and
          people above apply.

        

          
          
        

        

      


      

        
Stack coupons

        
Ring up several coupons in a row — each one comes off
          what's left, so percentages compound and a flat amount can't drop the
          check below zero. One coupon per comma: end with % for a
          percent off (e.g. 20%) or start with $ for a dollar
          amount off (e.g. $5). Bill, tip %, tax % and people above apply
          to the discounted check.

        

          
          
        

        

      


      

        
Tip food & drinks separately

        
Tip each part of the check at its own rate — e.g. 20% on
          food but less (or nothing) on the bar tab. One line per category as
          name amount tip%; name is optional. Tax % and people above
          apply to the combined bill.

        

          
          
        

        

      


      

        
Each diner's items & own tip

        
Everyone pays for exactly what they ordered and
          tips their own way. One line per diner: their items (commas for several),
          then add their tip rate as a trailing % (e.g.
          40, 12.5 @20%). Tax % above is shared, split by what each
          ordered.

        

          
          
        

        

      


      

        
Split a tab by time present

        
A rolling tab (a bar, an hourly room, a metered cab) split
          by how long each person was there. The cost is shared, moment by
          moment, only among whoever was present. One diner per line as
          start-end; add more stretches with commas
          (e.g. 0-120, 150-180). Bill, tip %, tax % above apply.

        

          
          
        

        

      


      

        
Tax food & drinks separately

        
Tax each part of the check at its own rate — e.g. the bar
          tab taxed higher than the food. One line per category as
          name amount tax%; name is optional. The tip % and people above
          apply to the combined bill.

        

          
          
        

        

      


      

        
Tip by bill size (tiered)

        
Scale the tip to how big the check is. One bracket per
          line as up_to tip% (e.g. 50 18); the LAST line may be
          just a tip% for the open-ended "and above" tier. Bill, tax %
          and people above apply.

        

          
          
        

        

      


      

        
Who pays whom

        
One friend covered the bill? Enter what each person has
          already put in (one amount per person, in order) and get the fewest
          payments that square everyone up. Bill, tip %, tax % and people above
          apply.

        

          
          
        

        

      


      

        
Spending summary

        
Tracking several visits? Enter one bill per line as
          bill tip% [tax%] (e.g. 100 20 8) and get the
          totals, averages and your blended tip rate across them all.

        

          
          
        

        

      


      

        
Separate checks

        
Each party at the table wants its own check? Enter one
          check per line as bill tip% [tax%] [people] (e.g.
          60 20 8 2) and each gets its own tax, tip and fair split,
          plus the one table total the restaurant sees.

        

          
          
        

        

      


      

        
Server tip-out

        
Working a shift? Enter your net sales and the
          tips you collected, then list the support roles you tip out —
          one per line as role percent (e.g. busser 3).
          By default each share is a percent of sales; switch the basis to figure
          it on your tips instead. You keep whatever is left.

        

          
          
          
        

        

          
          
        

        

      


      

        
Happy hour

        
Some items marked down while the rest stay full price?
          Enter one item per line as name, price, [discount], [percent|amount]
          (e.g. Margarita, 12, 50 for half price, or
          Nachos, 9 for no discount). Each line's discount comes off
          that item alone, then tax and tip are figured on the discounted
          subtotal and split fairly. Uses the bill's tip %, tax % and people
          above.
